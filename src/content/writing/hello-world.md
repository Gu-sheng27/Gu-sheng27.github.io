This is the first thing published here, so it should probably say what this place is.

It is a reading space, not a feed. Pieces go up when they are finished, in no
particular rhythm, and they stay as published — corrections show up as an
"Updated" date under the title rather than as a silent edit.

There is no comment section, no analytics pixel, no newsletter popup, and no
cookie banner, because none of those things are here. If you want to reply,
email works.

## What belongs here

Anything worth writing down that is longer than a note and shorter than a book:
notes on building software, on tools that earned their place, on things
learned the hard way. The bar is not "is this impressive" but "would I want to
read this again in three years".

## What does not

Rapid reaction to news, which ages badly. Restatements of documentation, which
already exist. Anything written to fill a publishing schedule.

The first real piece is [Why this site is built this way](/writing/why-this-site/),
which explains the stack and the visual direction if you are curious about the
machinery underneath.
